## Medicash

Medicash is Rupert's health cash plan through Aristocrat. It pays back everyday healthcare costs
(dentist, optician, physio, consultant appointments and so on) up to a yearly limit for each type
of treatment. Aristocrat pays for it. It's treated as a benefit in kind, so it may be taxed. Part
of [[Work Benefits]].

### How to claim

- **Deadline:** within **26 weeks** of paying in full or finishing the treatment (or of the
  accident or the hospital discharge). The claim counts against the benefit period in which the
  treatment happened.
- **Pay in full first.** Nothing paid with loyalty points counts. Pre-paid treatment that hasn't
  happened yet doesn't count.
- **The receipt must be a fully paid original** showing:
  - the practitioner's name, address and qualifications
  - the date of treatment
  - the patient's name and address
  - a breakdown and description of the treatment
- **Not accepted:** invoices, card-machine slips, joint receipts, photocopies, estimates, or
  receipts that don't describe the treatment.
- UK treatment only. Nothing given by immediate family. Nothing paid through the employer.
- If another insurer also covers the treatment, tell Medicash. Medicash only pays its share.
- Medicash pays into a UK bank account.

### Rules worth knowing for each benefit

- **Routine dental:** check-ups, treatment, hygienist, X-rays, dentures, braces. Not whitening,
  cosmetic work, missed appointments, or dental plan fees paid by the employer.
- **Dental accident:** only for a direct external blow to the head or face. **You must have an
  emergency dental appointment within 10 days of the accident.** It needs the Medicash Dental
  Accident claim form and a report and treatment plan from the dentist. Damage from eating doesn't
  count.
- **Optical:** eye tests, prescription glasses, contact lenses and sunglasses, frames fitted with
  prescription lenses, and laser eye surgery. Not frames alone, repairs, or deposits or part
  payments.
- **Specialist consultations:** the consultant must be on the GMC Specialist Register. It covers
  the consultation fee only. Tests fall under the scans part of the same pot. Room, surgery and
  medicine fees aren't covered.
- **Diagnostic tests and scans:** they must follow from a GP or consultant appointment. Not home
  test kits, or scans for family history when you have no symptoms.
- **PMI excess:** needs the original documents from the PMI insurer (AXA) showing the excess you
  paid. Co-pay policies and employer-paid excesses aren't covered.
- **Complementary and alternative therapies, and chiropody:** the practitioner must be qualified
  and insured. The therapy must be in person, not by phone or video. General fitness sessions
  don't count.
- **Prescriptions, inoculations and flu jabs:** covers "prescription charges", plus jabs for flu,
  hepatitis A/B, typhoid, tetanus, yellow fever, rabies, malaria and a few others.
- **Health screening:** a private health check in one in-person appointment that includes at
  least blood pressure, cholesterol and diabetes, kidney or liver function, and a physical exam.
  Not home kits, or screens for work, visas or insurance.
