### How the benefits fit together

- **AXA excess:** Medicash pays up to £250 a year towards an excess Rupert pays to use his private
  medical insurance (AXA). It needs AXA's documents showing the excess. It won't pay an excess
  that the employer pays.
- **Dental plan fees:** up to half the Medicash routine-dental allowance (£62.50 of £125) can go
  towards dental care plan fees, but only if Rupert pays those fees himself.
- **Employer-paid exclusion:** Medicash won't pay for anything "arranged, paid or facilitated
  through your employer or another employee", including salary sacrifice.
