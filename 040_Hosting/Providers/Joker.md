## Joker Mailbox (an email option)

- About $12 a year per Basic mailbox (2 GB, 25 aliases). Plus is 10 GB with 300 aliases; its price wasn't found.
- IMAP/SMTP (`imap.joker.email`, `smtp.joker.email:587`). SMTP relay can send as any of your addresses, so it covers Gmail "send as".
- SPF and DKIM are set up automatically, there's spam filtering, and optional two-factor login.
- Charged per mailbox, so 12 mailboxes would cost about as much as DreamHost. It only works out cheaply as one or two mailboxes with aliases, with the forwards on Cloudflare Email Routing. See [[Hosting Email]].
