# Record of Terms — Framework

The things we need to decide and agree before a Record of Terms can be written and signed. No figures or proposals: just the questions. The worked draft is [[Record of Terms - Draft]].

---

## 1. Basis

- What date are our disclosure figures (D81) taken at?
- Have we each had the chance to take legal advice?
- Do we each have a solicitor reviewing it? Who?

## 2. The house

- Sell, or does one of us keep it (buyout)?
- If sold: before or after the subsidence repairs?
- Accept a cash settlement from the insurer instead of repairs, if offered?
- What triggers putting it on the market?
- What's the longstop date if repairs are delayed or never happen?
- Who chooses the estate agent?
- How is the asking price set?
- What offers are accepted, and who decides?
- Who chooses the conveyancing solicitor?
- Who lives there until the sale, and what are their responsibilities (upkeep, access, viewings)?

## 3. Sale proceeds

- What comes off the sale price before the split (mortgage, sale costs, excess, joint debts, anything else)?
- How is the remainder split: what percentages?

## 4. House costs until the sale

- Which costs are shared (mortgage, insurance, council tax, utilities, repairs, maintenance)?
- In what proportions?
- Does the split change at any point before the sale?
- Is anything paid now credited back from the sale proceeds?

## 5. Joint debts

- Which debts count as joint?
- How are repayments shared until the sale?
- Are they cleared from the sale proceeds or before?
- Rules on new borrowing and balance transfers?

## 6. Other assets and personal debts

- Bank accounts and savings: each keeps their own?
- Cars: each keeps their own?
- Personal debts: each responsible for their own?
- House contents: how are they divided?
- The cats?

## 7. Pensions

- Each keeps their own, or a pension sharing order?
- If sharing: whose pension, what percentage?
- What valuations is that based on, and are formal statements needed first?
- What happens if a formal valuation differs from the figures we've used?
- The Prudential trust: what signatures are needed?

## 8. Maintenance and clean break

- Spousal maintenance: none, or some (how much, how long)?
- Clean break: from when (the order, or completion of the sale)?
- Claims against each other's estates dismissed?
- Lucy: any contributions in the agreement, or kept outside it?

## 9. Insurance claims

- Subsidence: who deals with the insurer and GHG? Where does any payment go?
- Drain claim excess: who pays, if billed?

## 10. Practical

- Legal costs: who pays?
- Court fee: who pays?
- Which solicitor drafts the consent order? Who reviews it?
- When is the Final Order applied for?
- What happens if either of us dies before the sale?
- Anything else either of us wants recorded?
