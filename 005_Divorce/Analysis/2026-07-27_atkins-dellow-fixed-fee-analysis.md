# Atkins Dellow fixed fee — what £1,982 actually covers

## Question asked

Is the £1,982 payment-on-account a reasonable amount up front, given it's quoted as a fixed fee?
Specifically: if Alan does very little work, is Rupert exposed to paying for time never spent? And
is there a general right to cancel if Rupert isn't happy?

## Documents reviewed

- `Correspondence/heather/caldwell/2026-07-20_terms-of-business.pdf` (Atkins Dellow Terms of Business v1.2)
- `Correspondence/heather/caldwell/2026-07-20_cost-schedule.doc`
- Alan Caldwell's 20 Jul 2026 covering email (engagement pack) —
  [[2026-07-20_atkins-dellow-engagement-pack]]

## Key findings

1. **Genuinely fixed fee, not a time estimate.** ToB para 13: charges are set at the fixed fee "for
   the scope of work stated" — doing the work faster than expected does not entitle Rupert to a
   partial refund.
2. **But if the matter doesn't reach completion, billing reverts to reasonable time-based charges**
   (ToB paras 23, 29, 69) — the £1,982 is money "on account," offset against the final bill (para
   31, 40), so any unused balance in that scenario is refundable, not Atkins Dellow's to keep.
3. **Scope creep is gated** — if the matter becomes more complex/contested (reverting to hourly per
   Alan's covering email) or the agreed scope changes, they must notify in writing before charging
   more (para 27).
4. **14-day no-reason cancellation right** applies only if the contract is made **off their
   premises** (ToB paras 71–76, Consumer Contracts Regs 2013) — i.e. Rupert signs at home and
   posts/emails the Schedule back, rather than signing in their office. Decided 27 Jul: sign at
   home, scan, email — preserves the right; plan to visit the office in person was dropped for
   this reason. Payment can still be made by phone (Ways to Pay option 2 covers phone or in-person).

## Hours calculation

£1,982 is quoted "inclusive of VAT and disbursements." For this uncontested consent-order scope,
the only applicable disbursement is the £60 consent order court fee (the other listed disbursements
— £313 contested finances, £263 child arrangements, £190 applications within proceedings — don't
apply to this matter).

- £1,982 − £60 disbursement = £1,922 fee, inc. VAT
- Ex-VAT: £1,922 ÷ 1.2 = £1,601.67
- Alan Caldwell's rate: £300/hr + VAT = £360/hr inc. VAT
- **£1,922 ÷ £360 ≈ 5.34 hours** (≈ 53 six-minute billing units) of Alan's time equivalent

## Reasoning / conclusion

~5.3 hours of solicitor time plus a court fee, spread over 6+ months of drafting, correspondence
with Heather's solicitor, and court submission, is a plausible amount of real work for an
uncontested consent order — not "half an hour to fill in a form." The genuine protections already
exist in the contract (can't silently exceed scope; refundable on-account money if it stops early);
the £1,982 itself isn't unreasonable on its face.
