## Review findings

### Cards paragraph — the 84%/16% figure is correct, precisely sourced

Initially flagged as a possible error (finance project's own tracked
figures show a nominal 80/20 split), until Rupert clarified the framing:
the 84/16 isn't about the Pebmarsh-only shared costs — it's about *all*
of Rupert's housing costs (Pebmarsh share + his Westgate liability, which
Heather contributes nothing toward) versus Heather's Pebmarsh-only share.

Confirmed against Rupert's "Personal Finance" Google Sheet (the actual
source of truth — see [[reference_google_sheet]]):
- **Actual — Rupert £2,683.35 (84.04%) vs Heather £509.70 (15.96%)** —
  matches the "84%/16%" quoted in the draft exactly.
- **Difference vs a clean 80/20 split of the full combined pot: £128.91**
  — matches the "approximate £128 imbalance" in the draft exactly, and is
  the figure Rupert was comparing (as an aside, not a claim needing
  verification) to the Barclaycard's £132.42 interest charge that cycle.
- The sheet's own structure: Westgate costs (Mortgage/Rent, Council Tax,
  Insurance, Gas/Electricity/Oil, Water) split 50/50 with Mandy, combined
  with Pebmarsh costs at the current nominal 80/20 split, to produce the
  "Actual" all-in totals and percentages above.
- A finance-project-only estimate (using just the £500/mo Westgate rent
  liability, no other Westgate bills) landed at 83/17 — close but less
  precise than the sheet's 84.04/15.96, confirming the extra ~1 point
  comes from folding in the full Westgate bill split (council tax,
  insurance, gas/electric, water), not just rent.
- **Net: both figures in the draft are accurate and well-sourced, not
  estimates or rhetorical rounding.** No correction needed.

### Drains paragraph — checks out

- £400 excess ÷ 2 = £200 matches the drain claim on record (Ageas ref
  6866220H via Catalyst Services UK, repairs booked 28 Jul 2026 AM, £400
  excess collected by the contractor on the day).
- Correctly framed as *Rupert's assumption* that the split is 50/50,
  rather than stating it as already-agreed fact — appropriate given it
  doesn't appear to have been explicitly confirmed with Heather yet.

### Tone

Matches Rupert's established preference for these messages: direct,
businesslike, no padding — states facts and makes the ask plainly,
consistent with [[feedback_structure_over_detail]] and the tone notes
already logged in [[RBS Mortgage Notification]].

## Outcome

No changes needed to the draft — both quoted figures (84%/16%, £128
imbalance) are accurate and precisely sourced from the Google Sheet, and
the drains figure is correct. Message was considered ready to send as
drafted.
