# Mandy Zimmer Care and Support

Mandy's care business. The logo and the draft rate card live in the repo: `business/brand/` (`mandy-logo-trans.png` is the round badge with a transparent background, the preferred one; `mandy-logo-cropped.jpeg` is a square crop) and `business/rate-card.html` (one A4 page).

## Rate card (first draft, 14 Oct 2026)

Rupert wants the terms very basic and friendly, in Mandy's own voice, not legalistic.

- Monthly invoicing in arrears (as now), 14 days to pay by bank transfer
- One month's notice to finish, both ways, with flexibility for hospital or a move into care
- 45p a mile for driving clients or running errands (travel to and from the client's home included)
- Purchases passed on with receipts
- 24 hours to cancel a visit; notice of holidays
- Yearly rate review with a month's notice

**Placeholders:** rates £30 weekday / £34 evenings and weekends / £45 bank holidays, start date 1 Jan 2027, phone number. As of 8 Nov 2026 no rate change is expected soon, so the rate card and the letterhead to go with it are on hold.

## Charge-out rate research (14 Sep 2026)

Mandy charges about **£18–20 an hour**. For qualified, non-nursing personal care for private clients in Suffolk/Essex, 2026: self-employed typically £18–28; agencies £26–38; Helping Hands from £32.40; Homecare Association minimum price for 2026-27 £34.42. Recommended **about £30** (range £27–33), £33–35 evenings and weekends, £45 bank holidays, £35+ for dementia or complex care.
