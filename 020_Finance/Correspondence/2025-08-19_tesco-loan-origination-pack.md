## Why this document mattered

It settled a question the project had been answering by inference. The
£353.76 interest figure was already recorded, but its **provenance** was
unclear and no per-payment interest data existed anywhere — Banktivity holds
zero interest line items for this loan, and Tesco sends no loan statements by
email (only credit-card ones).

The agreement resolves it: the loan is **precomputed, not amortising**.
Interest is worked out once at the outset, added to the loan amount, and the
total divided by 24. There are no monthly interest charges to find, and a
per-payment interest/principal split **cannot** be derived from the APR — an
attempt to do so earlier in the 16 Sept 2026 session produced a plausible but
entirely fictitious table. A genuine breakdown is available free from Tesco on
request as an "Amortisation Table".

See [[Tesco Loan]] for the full absorbed detail, including the three distinct
balance figures, the corrected payment-date pattern, fees, and the
address-on-file flag.
