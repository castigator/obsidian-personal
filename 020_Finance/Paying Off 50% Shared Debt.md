## Options, cheapest-first (assuming ~£7,400)

1. **New 0% money transfer credit card** — e.g. Tesco Bank does 0% money
   transfers for 9 months (3.99% fee); market fee is typically ~4%.
   Cost ≈ £295 one-off if cleared within the promo. Risks: new-card credit
   limit may be below £7,400; needs discipline to clear (or re-transfer to a
   0% balance transfer card at promo end — that works, since the debt would
   then be in Rupert's name).
2. **Personal loan £7,500 over 2–3 years** — best-buy APRs ~5.7–5.9%
   (Tesco Clubcard 5.7%, M&S Bank 5.9% as of July 2026). Interest ≈ £450
   (2yr) / £680 (3yr). Fixed, certain, no promo cliff. Note £7,500 is a
   rate-band threshold — borrowing £7,500 is often cheaper than £7,000.
3. **Check existing cards for money-transfer offers** — NatWest (£8,279
   headroom) may have targeted offers; check the app. Existing Tesco card's
   standard-rate money transfer (3.99% fee + ~standard APR) is a last resort.
4. **Do nothing** — if the cards charge typical ~25% APR, Rupert's half
   accrues roughly £1,800/year. Strongly worth acting.
