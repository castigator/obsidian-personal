statement ([[2026-08-16_natwest-0354-statement.pdf]]), whose SUMMARY OF

| Balance | Monthly | Annual | Outstanding |

**How the 0% was lost** — straight off the statement's transaction list:
the £1,900 balance transfer from M&S on 28 Jul **paid off the protected
£1,420.56 0% balance in full** (taking the card £479.44 into credit), and
the £1,982 Atkins Dellow charge the same day then landed as **new purchase
debt** at the standard rate. The 0% intro offer wasn't lost to a rule or an
expiry — it was simply *repaid*, and the expensive borrowing was left
sitting here. The offer's original 16 Feb 2027 expiry is now irrelevant;
there is no promotional balance for it to apply to.

**The statement warned about this a month ahead** — "your estimated interest
payment next month is £53.99" — and the £52.55 duly landed on 15 Sept. The
warning was missed because the 17 Aug refresh only read NatWest's
figure-free "statement ready" email, not the statement itself. See
[[2026-08-16_natwest-statement-0pc-gone]].
