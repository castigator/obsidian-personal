## ✅ App access resolved (17 Sept 2026)

Capital One's web portal carries a banner that **web access is being turned
off** — *"Web access to your account is turning off soon. If you haven't
already, download the mobile app so you can manage your card."* (seen
17 Sept 2026). No cutoff date given.

Rupert had been unable to get into the mobile app, which would have left him
with no way to see this card at all once the web is withdrawn. **Resolved
the same day:** the app registration asks for the **16-digit card number and
expiry date**, taken off the physical card, and he is now in. No further
action — the web withdrawal is no longer a risk to visibility of this
account.
