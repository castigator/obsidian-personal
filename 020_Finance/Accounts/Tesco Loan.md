## Overpayments and early settlement

- **Overpayments:** allowed. Tesco reduces the **term** to match, not the
  monthly payment — so overpaying shortens how long you're paying, rather
  than lowering the £139.74/month.
- **Early settlement:** allowed. Requires requesting a **settlement
  estimate** from Tesco first (not a fixed/self-service payoff figure) —
  consistent with the CCA 1974 statutory right to early settlement with a
  rebate of future interest.
