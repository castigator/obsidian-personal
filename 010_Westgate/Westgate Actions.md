# Westgate Actions

Westgate keeps its to-dos inside each topic note's "Important and To Do" section (see [[19 Westgate]]), so this page collects them rather than holding its own list. Tick items here or in the topic note: both are the same checkbox. The westgate session loads these items at start; `/close` keeps each note's section current. They also appear on [[Claude Actions]] and in the daily note.
