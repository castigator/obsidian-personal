## Renters' Rights Act 2025 (in force 1 May 2026)

The Act abolished Section 21 no-fault evictions and converted all ASTs to assured periodic tenancies. Key impacts on this tenancy:

- **Landlord notice to sell (Ground 1A):** 4 months' notice (supersedes the 2-month contractual term)
- **Landlord/family occupation (Ground 1):** 4 months' notice
- **12-month protected period** from tenancy start — landlord cannot serve notice under Grounds 1 or 1A during the first year (i.e. before 22 Aug 2026)
- **Tenant notice to end:** 2 months under the new Act (increased from 1 month in the contract)

## Succession on Tenant Death

### Statutory position (Housing Act 1988, s.17)

If the sole tenant dies, the tenancy can pass by succession in this priority order:

1. **Surviving spouse or civil partner** — must have been occupying the property as their only or principal home at the time of death
2. **Cohabitant** — a person living with the tenant "as the tenant's wife or husband" — qualifies only if no spouse/civil partner meets the above test

### Application to this tenancy

- **Rupert** is the sole named tenant on the *original* 2025 agreement
- **[[Mandy Zimmer]]** (Rupert's partner) lives at the property but was not named on the original tenancy
- **Heather Thomas** (Rupert's legal wife, divorce pending) lives at Pebmarsh — she does not occupy Westgate as her principal home, so would almost certainly not qualify as a successor
- Mandy would therefore likely have qualified as a cohabitant successor, stepping into the tenancy on the same terms — but the **2026 joint agreement now names her directly**, which is the more secure route and the reason it was pursued (see [[Contract]])

### Risks and actions (historical — for the original single-tenant agreement)

- The original tenancy agreement did not contain a specific clause on death or succession
- The estate remains liable for rent until the tenancy is formally ended (by successor, executor, or landlord)
- An executor with no successor can end with 4 weeks' notice (NTL2 form)
- This risk is being resolved by adding Mandy as a named joint tenant — see the timeline below and [[Contract]] for current status
